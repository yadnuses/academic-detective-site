# 迁移地图 · 同学地图 · 结尾 讲稿

---

## 一、迁移地图

### 中文

大家现在看到的是范瑶珠家族三代人的迁徙地图。从 1931 年福建建瓯开始，到 1946 年 16 岁的范瑶珠独自乘船前往上海，再到 1953 年瞒着家人报名赴疆。这条线穿过了整个中国，从东南沿海到西北边疆。

**1948 年**，范瑶珠在上海伯特利护校实习时，护理了患肺结核的孙钢。她每次去查房都带一本小说，书页里夹着小纸条。没有手机，没有微信，一本书就是一座桥。

**1953 年**，范瑶珠瞒着家人报名支援新疆，坐火车到西安，再换乘敞篷卡车，历时 40 天抵达乌鲁木齐。途中有人半途返回，她与同伴约定：绝不做逃兵。

**1954 年**，孙钢放弃上海法院的晋升，搭上秦腔剧团的卡车追随她到新疆。单位长桌铺上床单，摆上水果和茶点，同事坐在秋千上参加婚礼。没有婚纱，只有一本上海产的丝绸封面相册。

**1963 年**，哥哥范国帧在哈密因脑溢血去世。他 1953 年被“工作需要”强留新疆，整整十年未能回家。范瑶珠对此终生愧疚——“害了哥哥”。

**1967 年**，乌鲁木齐发生武斗，12 岁的孙葛抱着 5 个月大的弟弟逃回上海，只带了两本相册、借来的奶粉和尿布。一个 12 岁的女孩在恐惧中选择了照片，因为照片是唯一不可替代的东西。

**1985 年**，第三代李宜斐出生，冰箱、卡通 T 恤、毛绒玩具同时出现在全家福中。照相从记录人生大事，变成了留住日常团聚。

**2000 年**，儿子孙晓舞从美国回疆探亲。从 1953 年因国家建设而分离，到 2000 年因个人发展而跨国，团聚始终是家庭影像的核心。

**2020 年**，孙钢去世，与范瑶珠合葬在乌鲁木齐东山生态园，面朝东方，他们来时的方向。

这条迁徙线，不是简单的地理移动，而是一个家庭在时代里的生存轨迹。三代人的命运，被一本书、一辆公交车、一张火车票、一本相册串成了一条回家的路。

### English

This is the migration map of Fan Yaozhu's family across three generations. It begins in 1931 in Jian'ou, Fujian. In 1946, sixteen-year-old Yaozhu left home alone for Shanghai. In 1953, she signed up for Xinjiang without telling her family. This line crosses all of China, from the southeast coast to the northwest frontier.

**1948**: During her internship at Nursing School in Shanghai, Yaozhu cared for Sun Gang, a tuberculosis patient. Every time she visited his ward, she brought a novel with small notes tucked inside. No mobile phones, no WeChat. A book became a bridge.

**1953**: Without telling her family, Yaozhu signed up to support Xinjiang. She took a train to Xi'an, then switched to an open truck. The journey took forty days. Some turned back halfway; she and her companions promised: we will not be deserters.

**1954**: Sun Gang gave up his promotion at the Shanghai court and followed her to Xinjiang on a Qinqiang opera troupe's truck. Their wedding was held on a long table covered with bedsheets, with colleagues sitting on swings. No wedding dress, only a silk-covered photo album made in Shanghai.

**1963**: Her brother Fan Guozhen died of a cerebral hemorrhage in Hami. He had been kept in Xinjiang by "work needs" since 1953, unable to return home for ten years. Yaozhu felt guilty for the rest of her life, believing she had "harmed her brother."

**1967**: Armed conflict broke out in Urumqi. Twelve-year-old Sun Ge fled back to Shanghai with her five-month-old brother, carrying only two photo albums, borrowed milk powder, and diapers. In fear, the girl chose photographs because they were the only irreplaceable things.

**1985**: The third generation, Li Yifei, was born. Refrigerators, cartoon T-shirts, and plush toys appeared in the family photo. Photography shifted from recording major events to preserving everyday reunions.

**2000**: Their son Sun Xiaowu returned to Urumqi from the United States for a family visit. From separation in 1953 for national construction to international migration in 2000 for personal development, reunion has always been the core of family imagery.

**2020**: Sun Gang passed away and was buried with Yaozhu in Urumqi, facing east, the direction they came from.

This migration line is not just geography. It is the survival trajectory of a family through time. Three generations are strung together by a book, a bus, a train ticket, and a photo album into one road home.

## 二、同学地图

### 中文

看完家族的迁徙，我们再来看看这个夏天的迁徙。

这张地图上，34 位同学从世界各地汇聚到南京。有人来自南京大学、中国科学技术大学、同济大学，有人来自南开、北航、浙大，也有人来自英国南安普顿大学、澳大利亚莫纳什大学。

每一个点，都是一个人的来处。每一个来处，都有一段自己的故事。我们从不同的专业、不同的城市、不同的时区出发，在这个夏天交汇于同一间教室。

这让我们想到，范瑶珠当年从福建到上海，从上海到新疆，也是从一个个坐标出发，最终把坐标变成了家。今天的我们，也在做同样的事。

### English

After seeing the family's migration, let us look at our own migration this summer.

On this map, thirty-four students have gathered in Nanjing from all over the world. Some are from Nanjing University, USTC, and Tongji. Some are from Nankai, Beihang, and Zhejiang University. Others come from the University of Southampton in the UK and Monash University in Australia.

Every point is someone's origin. Every origin carries its own story. We set out from different majors, different cities, and different time zones, and this summer, we meet in the same classroom.

It reminds us that Fan Yaozhu also started from one coordinate, then another, and finally turned coordinates into home. Today, we are doing the same thing.

回到我们暑校的主题，什么是可计算的，什么是不可计算的？
所有的内容是可计算的，但怎么讲述故事是不可计算的。
能够感恩彼此依然是人类的特长。
酷炫的视觉效果并不是表达的终点，真正珍贵的是故事里那些触动人的瞬间。

Returning to the theme of our summer school: what is computable, and what is not?
All content is computable, but how to tell a story is not.
The ability to be grateful for one another remains a uniquely human gift.
Dazzling visual effects are not the endpoint of expression — what is truly precious are the moments in the story that touch people's hearts.

## 三、结尾

### 中文

这就是我们的 presentation。

范瑶珠用 272 张照片为家族保留了 70 年的记忆。而我们用代码、地图和文字，把这些记忆重新激活。

One more thing，我们遇见了彼此。One last thing，这个夏天，我们从地图上的坐标，变成彼此记忆里的家人。

感谢观看。后会有期。

### English

This is the end of our presentation.

Fan Yaozhu used 272 photographs to preserve seventy years of family memory. We used code, maps, and text to bring those memories back to life.

One more thing, we met each other. One last thing, this summer, we turned from coordinates on a map into family in each other's memories.

Thank you for watching.
